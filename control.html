Control

While there exists attempts to tackle observation-based grasping policies using imitation learning and reinforcement learning, robust 6D grasping in cluttered scenes remain a challenging task. Model-based methods and imitation learning methods have been utilized to improve sampling efficiency and generalization of reinforcement learning such as Guided Policy Search and GAIL methods. In this project, I plan to investigate different algorithms to achieve natural and reactive policies that can be utilized in real world environments.
