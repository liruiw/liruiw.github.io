Grasp

Generating grasp poses is a crucial component for robot object manipulation tasks. Here we aspire to build a framework that optimize motion planning and grasp planning jointly. This planner would integrate the three main tasks needed for grasping an object: grasp synthesis, grasp selection, and motion planning. Previous trajectory optimization methods such as CHOMP offers goal set variant and GPMP2 offers fast replanning methods. Instead, we are interested in leveraging the diverse grasp set to plan in dynamic environment by online learning.
