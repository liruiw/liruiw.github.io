Lirui (Leroy) Wang

I am a researcher at OpenAI, working on physical AI and multimodal agents.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science from MIT. Before that, I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees in EE and CS at University of Washington and worked at NVIDIA.
