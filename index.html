Research Projects

Grasp Planning

A robust and generalized 6D grasping based on imcomplete observation remains an open problem despite numerous traditional and data-driven approaches. This project seeks to exploit geometry to build a pipeline that combine motion and grasp planning in cluttered scene. It models reachability-aware grasping as a trajectory optimization problem based on CHOMP planner. More Info: (Demo)

6D Object Pose Tracking with the Deep Iterative Matching Network

Pose estimation has been studied extensively by computer vision and robotics researchers. While direct pose regression from RGB image is limited by various factors, DeepIM (Deep Iterative Matching) takes an iterative approach to refine initial pose estimation by matching rendered image against real image. This project mainly addresses the performance of DeepIM on YCB dataset and tracking tasks. More Info: (Poster, PDF, Demo)



Intern Projects

Automated Drone and Vehicle Battle

Real-time strategy among mobile robots and drones can play a role in automated robot battle. In DJI, we deploy image processing, control and high-level state machines that enable the M100 drone to execute tasks such as landing and takeoff, communication and survelliance. The mobile vehicle, embedded with a TX1 and RGB camera, runs a SLAM for localization, a planner for finding enemies, and shoot the bullets, refilled by drone, with object detection.
