Joint Motion and Grasp Planning

We make three contributions in our paper. We introduce an integrate planner that models joint motion and grasp planning as an optimization problem. The computation of feasible and reachable grasps and the search for collision-free motions are connected within this framework. An online learning framework and a grasp synthesis approach are used to generate grasp and select grasp online. We showcase our algorithms in three domains in planning, simulation, and the real world.
